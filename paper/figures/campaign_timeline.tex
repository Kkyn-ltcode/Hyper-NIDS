\caption{Chronological shard structure and cross-campaign evaluation splits. Each rectangle represents one data shard. \textbf{Theia} (top): the model trains on Campaign~1 (shards 0--8) and is tested entirely on the unseen Campaign~2 (shards 12--24), separated by a 92-hour gap. \textbf{CADETS} (middle): 10 shards with attacks concentrated in later shards. \textbf{TRACE} (bottom): 50 strategically selected shards from 211 total ($\sim$200M of 835M events); dots mark selected shards. Small red bars indicate shards containing attack events. All splits are strictly chronological to prevent temporal data leakage.}
