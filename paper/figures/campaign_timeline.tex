\caption{Chronological shard structure and evaluation splits. Each rectangle represents one data shard. \textbf{Theia} (top): the model trains on Campaign~1 (shards 0--8) and is tested entirely on the unseen Campaign~2 (shards 12--24), separated by a 92-hour gap. \textbf{CADETS} (middle): 10 shards with attacks concentrated in later shards. \textbf{TRACE} (bottom): following prior work, we use the first 20 of 211 total shards ($\sim$74M events); the first attack wave begins at shard~8. Small red bars indicate shards containing crossprocess attack events. All splits are strictly chronological to prevent temporal data leakage.}
\label{fig:timeline}
